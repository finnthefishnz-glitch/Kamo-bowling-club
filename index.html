About Us

Welcome to Kamo Bowling Club! Established in 1967, we're proud to be a hub for social and competitive bowling in the Kamo community. Our club is open to all ages and skill levels. Whether you’re looking to compete or just have a roll-up, you’ll find a friendly welcome here.
